        Questions.

        Will my client see your name?

        No. Everything is built under your agency's brand, and you keep the client relationship. I can join a sales or kickoff call as your engineer if that helps you close.

        How does pricing work?

        One fixed price after a free scope call: 50% to start, 50% at launch. Phone minutes, AI usage and hosting are billed by those platforms directly to you or your client, and I estimate them up front.

        How long does a build take?

        Receptionists, chat agents and automations usually take one to two weeks. Custom CRMs and web apps take three to six. A working demo for a prospect takes about three business days.

        Who owns the code and accounts?

        You do. Repos and platform accounts are set up in your or your client's name from day one, with handoff docs.

        What if the AI says something wrong?

        Hard rules like prices, discounts and medical questions are checked in code, not just in the prompt. When it's unsure, it hands off to a person, and every call is logged so problems get fixed from real data.
